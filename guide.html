Guide

Historia is a collection of the historical flags of Europe, from medieval banners and royal standards through to each country's modern flag. ALSO ABOVE IS A TEST VIDEO JUST TO TEST EMBEDDING A REAL GUIDE VIDEO FOR LATER

  • Press Enter on the title screen to open the map page.
  • Use the country list on the left of the map page to pick a country. Typing in the search bar narrows the list down.
  • Each country page shows its modern flag at the top, then every historical flag in order, each with a description and its dates.
  • Scroll the middle of a country page to read on. Use Back to map at the top to return.
