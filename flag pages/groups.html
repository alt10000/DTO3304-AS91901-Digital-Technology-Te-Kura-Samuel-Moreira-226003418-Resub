Culture Groups

Compare flags by culture group

These pages look beyond single countries. Each one gathers the flags of a broader culture group, such as the Slavs, the Latins or the Nordic peoples, and compares their colours, symbols and histories side by side.

Latin and Romance PeoplesFrance, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Romania and the other lands where Latin gave rise to new languages. Germanic PeoplesGermany, Austria, the Low Countries and the German-speaking world. Nordic PeoplesDenmark, Sweden, Norway, Iceland and Finland, and the cross they all share. Baltic StatesEstonia, Latvia and Lithuania: three tricolours, one shared history. Celtic PeoplesIreland, Scotland and the Celtic lands of the Atlantic fringe. Finno-Ugric PeoplesFinns, Estonians and Hungarians: linked by language, not by a flag.
