Historical Flags

Medieval Banner of the Margraviate of Austria and Early Duchy of Austria (976–1191)

Associated with the early Babenberg rulers of Austria, this banner represents the earliest period of Austrian heraldic identity. Its exact design is uncertain, as the familiar red-white-red colours are not reliably documented until the early 13th century.

Flag of the Late Duchy and Archduchy of Austria (1191–1804)

This flag featured three horizontal stripes of red, white and red, becoming one of the oldest and most recognisable symbols of Austria. The design is traditionally associated with Duke Leopold V, although it was first reliably documented under Duke Frederick II around 1230.

Flag of the Austrian Empire (1804–1918)

Following the establishment of the Austrian Empire under Emperor Francis II in 1804, the black-and-yellow horizontal bicolour became the principal imperial flag. Its colours originated from the traditional dynastic colours of the House of Habsburg and represented the monarchy until its dissolution following the First World War.

Merchant Ensign of Austria-Hungary (1869–1918)

Introduced following the Austro-Hungarian Compromise of 1867, this maritime flag combined the Austrian red-white-red colours with the Hungarian red-white-green colours. It featured the crowned Austrian coat of arms near the hoist and the Hungarian coat of arms towards the fly, representing the dual monarchy's two constituent kingdoms.

State Flag of the First Austrian Republic (1919–1934)

Following the collapse of the Austro-Hungarian Empire in 1918, the newly established Austrian Republic adopted the traditional red-white-red triband as its national flag. The state flag featured the Austrian coat of arms, consisting of a crowned, single-headed black eagle, without the broken chains found on the modern coat of arms.

State Flag of the Federal State of Austria (1934–1938)

Following the establishment of the authoritarian Federal State under Engelbert Dollfuss, Austria introduced a new state flag in 1934. It retained the red-white-red design but featured a revised coat of arms depicting a black double-headed eagle with golden halos, reflecting the regime's emphasis on Austria's imperial and Catholic heritage.

Civil Flag and Ensign of Austria (1918–present, with interruptions)

The Austrian civil flag consists of three equal horizontal stripes of red, white and red, without any additional emblems or decorations. Adopted following the collapse of the monarchy in 1918, it was briefly discontinued during the Nazi annexation (1938–1945) before being restored after the Second World War as Austria's national flag.
