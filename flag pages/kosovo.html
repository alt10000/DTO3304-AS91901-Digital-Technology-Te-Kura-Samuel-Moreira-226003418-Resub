National Flag of Kosovo (2008–present)

Adopted on 17 February 2008, following Kosovo's declaration of independence, the flag features a golden silhouette of Kosovo on a dark blue field, with six white stars arranged in an arc above it. Designed by Muhamer Ibrahimi, the six stars represent Kosovo's six major ethnic communities: Albanians, Serbs, Bosniaks, Turks, Roma and Gorani.
